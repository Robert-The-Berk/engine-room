Chief engineer, report

The bridge is charging the jump drive. Keep every system powered until it is ready. Your engine room is on the left; the ship is on the right.

  • Walk to a console to set its power. Match what the system needs.
  • Too much power for a few seconds trips its breaker. Reset it at the breaker panel.
  • Too little power and the ship takes damage. Watch the ship to see it happen.
  • Swap spent fuel rods and coolant before they run dry.

Move with WASD or arrows, or click a station. Q/E lower or raise power, R matches the load, Space uses a station. P pauses.

    Engineer's manual
    • Consoles along the back wall set power for Shields, Weapons, Life support, Engines and Repair. On each console screen the bar is the power you set and the white mark is what the system needs right now.
    • Bridge alerts arrive a few seconds before the power need hits ("in 5s"). Use that time to get to the right console.
    • Breakers. A system can take up to 25% more than it needs (at least 4 MW extra). Above that, its breaker warns, then trips after about 2.5 seconds. When an alert ends and a system needs less, turn it down or it will trip. A tripped system gets no power until you reset it at the breaker panel.
    • Reactor. Total allocation can't exceed reactor output. Low coolant cuts output; an empty fuel rod shuts it down.
    • Refuelling. When the rod is below 40%, eject it at the fuel port (left side of the reactor). Drop it in the disposal chute, take a fresh rod from the fuel rack and load it. Coolant works the same way on the right side. Ejecting cuts power until the new one is in, so pick a quiet moment.
    • Repair needs power whenever the hull is damaged. Powered repair slowly restores the hull.
    • Repair bay (right wall) is offline for now. In a future update, broken ship parts can be brought here and repaired at the cost of power and time.
